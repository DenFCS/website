The Process
Intake Form → Staff/Athlete meeting→ Individualized Plan
Every athlete completes a fall intake form. Our staff sits down, reviews the data, and determines the right programs across lifting, throwing, hitting, and recovery. Our player development systems software generates an individualized plan — strength programs, throwing programs, throwing schedule, and workload management — all built around your goals. Velocity. Command. Power. Speed. The fall season is hyper-focused on hitting them.

— DenFCS Scholarships —

We serve players and communities.

DenFCS scholarships make pro-level training in Houston accessible to athletes who otherwise couldn't get in the door. Built by pro players, for the next generation that's earned the rep but not the room.

How it works
— Who We Support —

The athletes we back.

Three groups we prioritize. If you fit, you should apply.

01

Financial need.

Athletes whose families would feel the squeeze of a full membership but who are putting in the work to earn pro-level training.

02

High potential.

Players the staff sees real upside in — measurable jumps when given real coaching, real reps, and real measurement.

03

Underrepresented.

Communities and programs that don't usually get pro-level facilities or instruction. We want to change that.

— How It Works —

From application to first rep.

A simple, honest process. No long forms, no run-around.

01

Apply

Hit the Apply Now button. Tell us a bit about you — name, position, where you train now, and why a DenFCS scholarship would change things. A few sentences is enough.

02

Quick conversation

If we think you're a fit, we'll set up a 15-minute call or in-person at one of the facilities. We'll talk goals, schedule, what you've been doing.

03

Trial week

Selected applicants get a no-cost trial week to train at The Den. You see what the program is like; we see how you work.

04

Scholarship awarded

If both sides are in, you're in. We'll lay out the membership length, expectations, and how to make the most of it.

Send your application.
